About Us 🐶

At Tails & Trails, we're passionate about giving dogs the love, care and attention they deserve.

We offer dog walking and dog boarding for families who want their pets to stay happy, active and cared for while they're busy or away.

Every pup is treated with kindness, patience and care, with their safety and happiness always coming first.

What your pup can expect

  • 🐾 Fun walks and exercise
  • 🎾 Playtime and attention
  • 🥣 Meals following your instructions
  • 💧 Fresh water
  • 💚 A safe, caring environment
  • 📸 Updates for owners when requested

Our Services
